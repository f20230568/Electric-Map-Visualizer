The following gives the best drawings:
1. For under 1000 vertices, large and small trees each hold half the vertices.
2. For more than that, small trees are fixed to be less than 4 vertices. Large trees should have minimum size 9 and maximum size 30-55 for a nice drawing.
3. Boards should be drawn in groups of 1000 vertices for nice drawings.
4. The forest should be divided equally in terms of number of vertices and not trees so that boards are of near equal size.
